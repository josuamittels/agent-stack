# Audit Accessibility

> **Skill** · **experimental** · **v0.1.2** · updated 2026-11-14

## Purpose

Assess a supplied interface, infographic, document, media artifact, or other design against the accessibility requirements that apply to its medium and delivery.

## Inputs

Provide an identifiable artifact and enough access to inspect it. Include the purpose, audience, medium, platform, relevant constraints and output destination when known. Reuse existing context; consequential missing facts are clarified before dependent conclusions.

## Workflow

1. **Set the scope and criteria.** Identify the artifact, delivery context, applicable standard and evaluated units. A full web baseline uses WCAG 2.2 AA; focused and non-web reviews declare their selected criteria and adaptations.
2. **Inspect the available evidence.** Use rendered material, contrast measurements and relevant interaction or assistive-technology checks when available. A screenshot leaves behavior and semantics unverified.
3. **Record outcomes and actions.** Distinguish fulfilled, not fulfilled, unknown and justified not applicable criteria. Link barriers to concrete remediation and unknowns to verification.
4. **Fill the report.** Use the [modular report template](https://github.com/josuamittels/agent-stack/blob/main/src/skills/audit-accessibility/templates/report.md) to document scope, evidence, results and applicable medium sections. Save the matching audit.json register so the scores can be reproduced.

Applicable checks include structure and reading sequence, descriptive headings, labels and instructions, and consistent identification or navigation. Visual hierarchy alone does not prove semantic structure. Broader language and brand preferences remain advisory unless an applicable accessibility requirement is demonstrably unmet.

UI contrast checks invoke [Check Colour Contrast](/capabilities/disciplines/check-colour-contrast) for measured foreground/background pairs. Results account for the rendered text size and weight or the visual role of a control, state, or graphic. Checks cover relevant labels, placeholders, errors, icons, control boundaries, selected states, and focus indicators across scoped themes and states. Missing measurements remain verification gaps. Rendered visibility and legibility are inspected alongside numeric ratios, including small or thin text and enlargement.

## Result and limits

Receive report.md and audit.json with criterion outcomes, evidence, barrier severity, prioritized actions and two descriptive metrics: assessed fulfillment and assessment coverage. Neither metric establishes conformance. Advisory practices stay separate from required-criterion scores.

An audit authorizes inspection and recommendations. Implementation, publication and consequential live actions need their own authorization. Reports are saved in a fresh audit folder; previous reports are preserved.
