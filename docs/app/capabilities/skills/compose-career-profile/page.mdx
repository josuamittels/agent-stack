# Compose Career Profile

> **Skill** · **stable** · **v1.0.0** · updated 2026-11-04

## Purpose

Build a detailed, reusable career profile from documents and the person's reflections. Later application and career workflows can use it as context for the person's experience, capabilities, motivation, personality, and work preferences.

## Workflow

1. **Gather existing material.** Share any useful CV or résumé, LinkedIn profile, past motivation letters, education or project information, and personality or strengths assessments. Add general context and corrections. Dictation is welcome; polished application copy is not required.
2. **Describe education.** Explain what you studied, why you chose it, what you valued or found difficult, and relevant courses, projects, exchanges, languages, certificates, representation, associations, or extracurriculars.
3. **Tell stories about work and projects.** For each relevant experience, describe the context, what you owned and personally did, why it mattered, how you worked with others, challenges, outcomes, and what you learned or liked and disliked.
4. **Reflect on motivation and preferences.** Describe what drives or interests you, what gives or drains energy, what you care about, the work environments that suit you, and what you hope to learn or do next. Personality assessments can prompt reflection; your own examples take priority.
5. **Review the story and clarify gaps once.** The skill checks chronology, role boundaries, personal versus team contributions, evidence for outcomes, and connections between experience and motivation. If a consequential gap remains, it asks one compact set of clarifying questions, then marks anything still unknown.
6. **Return the profile.** Receive a completed Markdown file based on the [career-profile template](https://github.com/josuamittels/agent-stack/blob/main/src/skills/compose-career-profile/templates/career-profile.md), either in the conversation or at a requested path.

The profile distinguishes documented facts, personal reports, and interpretations. It preserves uncertainty and limits sensitive details to what is relevant. Treat it as reusable context; verify dates, titles, credentials, and claims against original records before using them in a formal application.

## Inputs and result

Use any combination of source documents and answers gathered in conversation. The workflow skips questions already answered by reliable material, encourages expansive stories without steering the person's conclusions, and does not require every template field to be known.

The result is a structured profile covering formal details, languages, education, experience and projects, capabilities, motivation, personality, work preferences, career direction, evidence, conflicts, and open questions.
