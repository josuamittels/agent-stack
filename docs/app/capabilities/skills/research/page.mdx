# Research

> **Skill** · **experimental** · **v0.13.0** · updated 2026-10-31

## Purpose

Use Research to produce an appraised research report or literature review with traceable evidence, citations and repeatable working artifacts.

## Workflow

1. **Set the brief.** Record the question, scope, audience, language, depth, journal rules, citation style and requested outputs in a persistent project directory.
2. **Discover and qualify sources.** Use [Research Literature](/capabilities/disciplines/research-literature) for scholarly discovery and verification. Account for supplied papers and authors; add planned primary or official sources before the final qualification gate. Deep research also checks references from qualified initial papers.
3. **Appraise and structure the evidence.** Inspect sources, record findings and limitations with locators, map supporting and contrary evidence to claims, and assign sections and word budgets.
4. **Draft the prescribed sections.** Use [Natural Writing](/capabilities/disciplines/natural-writing) with section purposes, source and claim assignments, citation keys, terminology and neighboring context. Resolve missing support before accepting prose.
5. **Assemble, review and polish.** Assemble sections into the canonical draft, review argument and citation support, and polish academic prose while preserving evidence and qualifications. Refresh the summary and title against the finished body. Allow at most two substantive repair passes; report remaining blockers.
6. **Compile and deliver.** Format citations with Pandoc, generate the cited-source inventory, export requested formats and complete the objective checks. Chat delivery uses the compiled Markdown without another writing pass.

## Choose depth and document type

| Depth | Cited sources | Body words | Discovery |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quick | 8–12 | 800–1,600 | Initial searches and verification |
| Normal, the default | 16–24 | 2,000–4,000 | Initial searches and verification |
| Deep | 32–64 | 6,000 or more | Initial searches plus one backward-citation round |

Explicit requirements override these defaults. Source targets never justify padding or weakening eligibility. Journal selection is a separate choice and defaults to the Default preset.

Choose **research report** for an executive summary, question and scope, search and selection methods, findings and evidence synthesis, discussion and implications, limitations and unresolved questions, conclusion, and references. Choose **literature review** for a summary, review question and scope, search and selection methods, evidence overview, thematic synthesis, disagreements and evidence gaps, interpretation and limitations, conclusion, and references. Topic-specific subsections belong inside these fixed structures.

## Inputs and outputs

Provide a question and optionally an audience, scope, depth, journal rules, papers, citations, authors, language, style and output formats. Supplied works receive explicit inclusion, exclusion or unresolved outcomes; author suggestions receive targeted searches. They remain subject to eligibility and verification rules.

The default is a research report in chat with APA 7 citations. Request Markdown, editable LaTeX, Word or PDF when needed. Pandoc is required for citation compilation and conversion; PDF uses the documented bundled LibreOffice route through Word. Other citation styles require a local CSL file. The offline helpers make no network or model calls. Read the [CLI and dependency guide](https://github.com/josuamittels/agent-stack/blob/main/skills/research/references/cli.md) before running commands.

Working artifacts persist for repeatability. Only requested outputs and relevant limitations are delivered. Bibliographic verification and mechanical checks do not establish scientific correctness; Research retains responsibility for appraisal and claim support.
