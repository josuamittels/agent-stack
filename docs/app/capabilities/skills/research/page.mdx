# Research

> **Skill** · **experimental** · **v0.8.0** · updated 2026-10-28

## Purpose

Use Research to produce a source-grounded, critically appraised research report at the depth and format you request.

## Workflow

1. **Set the research question and depth.** Clarify the question, audience, intended use, scope, and depth so the evidence can answer the actual decision.
2. **Choose and document a method.** Select suitable sources, search and screening rules, and an appraisal approach; record limits and assumptions.
3. **Find and appraise evidence.** Search, screen, deduplicate, read, and assess sources against the chosen method. Use [Research Literature](/capabilities/disciplines/research-literature) for academic record searches when useful.
4. **Build the synthesis.** Compare findings, evidence quality, uncertainty, and disagreement before drafting conclusions.
5. **Write and check the report.** Present a traceable answer for the requested audience and format, then verify source links and the report’s objective requirements where applicable.

## Inputs

Use the topic or question, intended audience and use, requested deliverable or format, language, any supplied sources or constraints, and desired research depth. Ask through [ask-user-question](/capabilities/disciplines/ask-user-question) only for a missing choice that would materially change what evidence can answer the question. First use [think-harder](/capabilities/disciplines/think-harder) if context or evidence might resolve the choice; otherwise state a reasonable assumption and proceed.

## Result

Return the requested report in the requested format or save it to the requested location. If neither is specified, deliver the report in the conversation. Include a concise methods note, in-text citations and a reference list, the selected depth and journal selection, and the objective evaluation result with failed or unverified checks. For a material access or search limit, identify what was inaccessible and how it affects the conclusion. Do not expose internal chain-of-thought; present the research decisions, evidence, and reasoning needed to audit the report.
