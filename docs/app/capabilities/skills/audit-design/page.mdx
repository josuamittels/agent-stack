# Audit Design

> **Skill** · **experimental** · **v0.2.0** · updated 2026-11-12

## Purpose

Combine usability and accessibility assessment with a deeper aesthetic critique of a supplied design, while preserving its intended character.

## Inputs

Provide an identifiable artifact and enough access to inspect it. Include the purpose, audience, medium, platform, relevant constraints and output destination when known. Reuse existing context; consequential missing facts are clarified before dependent conclusions.

## Workflow

1. **Frame the design.** Share the artifact, scope, audience, purpose and any brand direction or references. The workflow inspects rendered evidence and records assumptions and limits.
2. **Review user experience and access.** [Audit UX](/capabilities/skills/audit-ux) performs the task-centered review and calls [Audit Accessibility](/capabilities/skills/audit-accessibility). Accessibility runs through UX once.
3. **Examine aesthetic logic and craft.** Evaluate applicable relationships in intent, composition, coherence, craft, distinction and restraint. Classify changes as corrections, refinements or optional explorations.
4. **Reconcile and report.** Receive a flexible parent critique and one action plan that preserves technical outcomes and resolves conflicts between expression, tasks and access. The parent critique has no fixed template or beauty score.

## Result and limits

Receive report.md for the parent critique, ux/report.md, and ux/accessibility/report.md with its audit.json register. The reports stay separate while the parent action plan links their findings. Missing required helper results leave the overall review partial.

An audit authorizes inspection and recommendations. Implementation, publication and consequential live actions need their own authorization. Reports are saved in a fresh audit folder; previous reports are preserved.

## Related capabilities

Choose [Audit Accessibility](/capabilities/skills/audit-accessibility) for accessibility alone, [Audit UX](/capabilities/skills/audit-ux) for user and task usability, or [Audit Design](/capabilities/skills/audit-design) for the coordinated review with deeper aesthetics. The [Skill Issue router](/capabilities/router/skill-issue) selects the narrowest matching workflow.
