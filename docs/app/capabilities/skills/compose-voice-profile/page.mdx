# Compose Voice Profile

> **Skill** · **experimental** · **v0.2.0** · updated 2026-10-28

## Purpose

Use Compose Voice Profile to create a reusable, evidence-backed voice guide for a brand or author.

## Workflow

1. **Set the profile goal.** Name the brand or author, audience, purpose, language and locale, intended users, current or aspirational direction, and where the finished profile should go.
2. **Use the voice-profile template.** Fill the fields and tables in the [voice-profile template](https://github.com/josuamittels/agent-stack/blob/main/skills/compose-voice-profile/templates/voice-profile.md); keep its section order and mark unknown details instead of guessing.
3. **Review the available evidence.** Separate direct instructions, approved brand guidance, writing samples, audience research, market evidence, and visual references. Keep current practice distinct from intended direction.
4. **Synthesize the strategy.** Use [Think Deeper](/capabilities/disciplines/think-deeper) to reconcile audience fit, product promise, distinctiveness, language, and the evidence. Pass it the source inventory, constraints, conflicts, and uncertainties.
5. **Write and review the profile.** Turn the supported strategy into practical voice, tone, structure, vocabulary, and channel guidance. Apply [Natural Writing](/capabilities/disciplines/natural-writing) and the relevant [language guide](/guidelines-and-references/guidelines/languages) before returning the completed Markdown.

## Inputs

Use your stated goal and any available details about:

- **Brand or author:** identity, purpose, values, positioning, personality, and existing brand guidance.
- **Product and topic:** offer, differentiators, claims, use cases, and subject matter to cover.
- **Audience:** user research, audience preferences, needs, expertise, objections, and feedback.
- **Market:** category conventions, competitors, alternatives, and credible research about points of difference.
- **Place and language:** country or region, target language, locale, cultural context, spelling, terminology, and market variants.
- **Expression:** channels, visual identity or references, current writing samples, admired examples, and explicit do/don't instructions.
- **Deliverable:** profile purpose, intended users, output language, requested depth, and file destination if specified.

## Result

Receive a complete Markdown voice profile with the template sections filled, supporting evidence identified, and unknowns or conflicts made clear. The skill returns it in the requested path or in the conversation.
