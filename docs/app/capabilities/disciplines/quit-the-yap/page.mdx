# Quit The Yap

> **Discipline** · **experimental** · **v0.3.0** · updated 2026-10-28

## Purpose

Use Quit the Yap to make an in-chat answer concise while preserving the information needed to answer the request.

## Workflow

1. **Identify the answer.** Find the direct response and any necessary qualification or next action.
2. **Remove what does not help.** Cut repetition, preamble, and details that do not change the reader’s understanding or decision.
3. **Return the concise answer.** Keep the tone natural and the requested information intact.

## Inputs

Use your latest request, relevant conversation context, established facts, and the answer or action the caller needs to communicate. Before drafting, invoke [Natural Writing](/capabilities/disciplines/natural-writing) as the required writing method. Pass the answer's context and ask it to apply English Absolute style. Natural Writing selects and applies the English language guidance, including its Absolute style section; do not read the English guideline separately here.

## Result

Return the requested answer in the requested format. Keep it direct and complete. If a necessary fact is missing, state the specific gap and ask only for the information needed to continue.
