# Mind Wandering

> **Discipline** · **experimental** · **v0.2.0** · updated 2026-10-28

## Purpose

Use Mind Wandering to explore creative directions that can help you return to a settled task with useful options.

## Workflow

1. **Set the creative boundary.** Keep the original goal and constraints visible while identifying what kind of exploration could help.
2. **Explore distinct directions.** Use relevant creative moves to generate varied ideas without treating novelty as a reason to expand the task.
3. **Return useful options.** Select the ideas that can inform the original work and hand them back with their relationship to the goal clear.

## Inputs

Use the question or brief, intended audience or experience, current stage, existing material, earlier attempts, constraints, available formats and tools, and any time or cost limit. Recover these from the task; do not turn an open invitation to play into an intake interview.

## Result

Return the creative material in the form the user requested. Include the selected direction or contrasting alternatives, their relevant differences, and important assumptions or unresolved questions when those help the next decision. A requested finished artifact remains the primary deliverable.
