# Natural Writing

> **Discipline** · **experimental** · **v0.6.0** · updated 2026-10-28

## Purpose

Use Natural Writing to draft, edit, or translate prose that reads clearly and naturally for its intended audience.

## Workflow

1. **Read the request and source.** Identify the audience, purpose, required voice, factual claims, and content that must remain unchanged.
2. **Revise the prose.** Use direct, concrete language and preserve meaning, evidence, and the requested format.
3. **Check the result.** Remove awkward phrasing and unsupported additions, then return the text in the requested form.

## Inputs

Apply this sequence before the clarification instructions below.

## Result

Return the requested text or artifact in its required format. Omit internal notes and voice analysis. Preserve any surrounding application or artifact structure required by the task. Do not claim human authorship or a detector guarantee.
