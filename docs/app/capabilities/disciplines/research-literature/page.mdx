# Research Literature

> **Discipline** · **experimental** · **v0.12.0** · updated 2026-10-31

## Purpose

Use Research Literature to find journal-filtered academic works across scholarly databases and return verified bibliographic records, available abstracts and a consistent source list. Use [Research](/capabilities/skills/research) to appraise papers, synthesize evidence and write a report.

## Workflow

1. **Resolve the search contract.** Record the question, depth, source targets, journal rules and other inclusion criteria. Preserve each supplied paper, citation or author under a request ID.
2. **Search eligible databases.** Use documented journal-restricted queries in OpenAlex, Crossref, Semantic Scholar or PubMed as coverage and access permit. Use publication-filtered Google Scholar as a supplementary route. Providers without usable journal restrictions, including arXiv, support identity or version verification while the filter is active.
3. **Resolve identities and verify records.** Deduplicate confirmed identities and compare dated retrieved metadata against a second scholarly database or the primary publisher. A reachable link alone does not verify a source.
4. **Expand references in deep mode.** Freeze qualified initial works, retrieve their available reference lists and screen overlapping references first. Expand one hop only; record unavailable lists, unresolved identities and truncation.
5. **Apply the final qualification gate.** Check required fields, identity verification, relevance, journal eligibility, discovery annotations and supplied-input outcomes. Exclude failed or unresolved records from the qualifying count and generate the readable list from the qualified inventory.
6. **Top up once if necessary.** If the qualified count is below the minimum, perform one targeted search pass and rerun the gate. Return the qualifying remainder with an honest shortfall if still below target. Do not relax criteria or add unhelpful works to fill a quota.

## Depth and source targets

| Depth | Default qualified-source target | Citation expansion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quick | 8–12 | None |
| Normal, the default | 16–24 | None |
| Deep | 32–64 | One backward-reference round |

Aim exploration at the upper bound; the lower bound is sufficient when further searches yield no helpful qualifying work. Explicit requested counts take precedence. Discovery-pool counts and the number eventually cited in a report are separate requirements.

## Journal selection

Journal selection remains independent of depth. The CSV-backed presets accept either metric at its threshold: Lenient uses article influence 1 or percentile 60; Default uses 2.5 or 80; Strict uses 5 or 90. Explicit request rules take precedence.

In deep mode, a relevant reference cited by at least two distinct qualified initial works may bypass the journal filter. The recorded resolved citation edges must support that exception. References cited by only one initial work still need ordinary journal eligibility. Other inclusion rules remain in force. Secondary discovery level describes how a work was found, not its study type or quality. New additions and top-up results never become expansion seeds.

## Inputs and results

Supply a question and optionally papers, citations, authors, date ranges, work types, language, access requirements, journal constraints or result targets. Exact works receive an explicit outcome; author suggestions trigger targeted searches without silently restricting the whole search to those authors.

Receive the qualified inventory, a standardized Markdown list, separate unresolved or disqualified records with reasons, and a verification receipt. The handoff records actual searches, provider provenance, eligibility decisions, supplied-input outcomes, reference coverage, count status and whether the single top-up was used. Raw retrieval records remain preserved. Verification establishes bibliographic identity; it does not establish study quality or claim support.

The [offline gate CLI](https://github.com/josuamittels/agent-stack/blob/main/skills/research-literature/references/qualification-gate.md) also works independently of Research's writing workflow. Caller-requested intermediate pool handoffs defer the final count check until the combined source pool is ready.
