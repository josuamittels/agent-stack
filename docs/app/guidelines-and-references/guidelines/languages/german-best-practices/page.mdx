# German Best Practices

> **Reference** · **experimental** · **v0.1.0** · updated 2026-10-28

## Overview

Comprehensive conventions and examples for clear, idiomatic, natural German prose across general, academic, technical, direct, article, and narrative writing.

## How to use this guide

Natural German makes the intended meaning available without unnecessary effort. Its vocabulary, grammar, sentence movement, and organization fit the subject, reader, situation, and form. It can be formal, conversational, terse, analytical, or literary. Native phrasing does not require bureaucratic distance, conspicuous informality, or elaborate sentences.

Use the topics below when you create or review work in this area. Follow your repository’s conventions where they are more specific.

## Topics

- Meaning, reader, and register
- Evidence, specificity, and uncertainty
- Organization and paragraph development
- Sentence structure and syntax
- Vocabulary and expression
- Rhythm and coherence
- Punctuation and presentation
- Style-specific conventions
