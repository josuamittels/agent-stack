# French Best Practices

> **Reference** · **experimental** · **v0.1.0** · updated 2026-10-28

## Overview

Comprehensive conventions and examples for clear, idiomatic, natural French prose across general, academic, technical, direct, article, and narrative writing.

## How to use this guide

Natural French makes the intended meaning available without unnecessary effort. Its vocabulary, grammar, sentence movement, and organization fit the subject, reader, situation, and form. It can be formal, conversational, concise, analytical, or literary. Native phrasing does not require elaborate abstraction, metropolitan slang, ceremonial introductions, or a uniformly elegant surface.

Use the topics below when you create or review work in this area. Follow your repository’s conventions where they are more specific.

## Topics

- Meaning, reader, and register
- Evidence, specificity, and uncertainty
- Organization and paragraph development
- Sentence structure and syntax
- Vocabulary and expression
- Rhythm and coherence
- Punctuation and presentation
- Style-specific conventions
