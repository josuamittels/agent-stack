# Composition

> **Reference** · **experimental** · **v0.3.0** · updated 2026-11-11

## Overview

Comprehensive composition, layout, and grid reference for spatial meaning, visual hierarchy, guidance, balance, rhythm, blank space, density, responsive systems, editorial design, interfaces, and production.

## How to use this guide

Composition gives content a spatial argument. Position, scale, alignment, separation, and sequence tell people what belongs together, what matters, and how to proceed. The same words can read as an instruction, a footnote, an endorsement, or a warning when their relationships change. Layout therefore participates in meaning before anyone finishes reading.

Use the topics below when you create or review work in this area. Follow your repository’s conventions where they are more specific.

For chart, map, and diagram decisions, consult [Infographics](/guidelines-and-references/guidelines/aesthetics/infographics) for encoding, scales, and truthful abstraction. This guide retains its own domain authority.
