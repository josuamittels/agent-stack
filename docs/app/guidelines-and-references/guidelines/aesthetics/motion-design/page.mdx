# Motion Design

> **Reference** · **experimental** · **v0.3.0** · updated 2026-11-11

## Overview

Comprehensive motion and animation reference for perception, timing, spacing, easing, springs, gesture physics, choreography, expressive identity, transitions, patterns, accessibility, motion systems, implementation, and evaluation.

## How to use this guide

Motion makes change perceptible. It can show what caused an event, where something went, how objects relate, what remains available, and whether a system responded. It also gives a composition temperament: hesitant, precise, buoyant, severe, restless, ceremonial. The same endpoints can communicate different things through the movement between them.

Use the topics below when you create or review work in this area. Follow your repository’s conventions where they are more specific.

For chart, map, and diagram decisions, consult [Infographics](/guidelines-and-references/guidelines/aesthetics/infographics) for encoding, scales, and truthful abstraction. This guide retains its own domain authority.
