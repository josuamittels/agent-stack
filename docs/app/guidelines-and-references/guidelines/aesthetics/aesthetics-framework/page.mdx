# Aesthetics Framework

> **Reference** · **experimental** · **v0.2.0** · updated 2026-11-11

## Overview

Shared design-reference framework: domain ownership, selective reading paths, decision priorities, and evidence for AI-assisted design across interfaces and other media.

## How to use this guide

Use these references to make informed design decisions. Each guide owns one domain; together they explain how behavior, form, perception, and delivery support an experience. They provide judgment and constraints rather than a mandatory visual style or a workflow for executing every project.

Use the topics below when you create or review work in this area. Follow your repository’s conventions where they are more specific.

## Topics

- Choose the reference by the decision
- Read selectively and follow dependencies
- Resolve decisions in context
- Coordinate domains without duplicating their rules
- Match evidence to the claim

For chart, map, and diagram decisions, consult [Infographics](/guidelines-and-references/guidelines/aesthetics/infographics) for encoding, scales, and truthful abstraction. This guide retains its own domain authority.
