# Sound Design

> **Reference** · **experimental** · **v0.2.0** · updated 2026-10-28

## Overview

Reference for understanding, composing, synthesising, shaping, implementing, and judging sound, including perception, emotion, musical structure, and audio for technology products.

## How to use this guide

Sound gives events a material character, organises attention through time, and suggests relationships between bodies, places, actions, and feelings. Its meaning emerges from the signal, the listener, and the situation. A technically clean sound can be emotionally empty; a rough recording can be exactly right.

Use the topics below when you create or review work in this area. Follow your repository’s conventions where they are more specific.
