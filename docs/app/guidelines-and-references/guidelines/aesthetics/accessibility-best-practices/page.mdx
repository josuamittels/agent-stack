# Accessibility Best Practices

> **Reference** · **experimental** · **v0.2.0** · updated 2026-10-28

## Overview

Comprehensive accessibility reference for inclusive product decisions, EU legal scoping, WCAG requirements, visual and interaction design, content, implementation, assistive technology, design systems, evaluation, and ongoing operations.

## How to use this guide

Accessibility lets people with disabilities perceive information, understand choices, operate controls, and complete tasks with the independence and privacy available to other people. It belongs to the whole service: its interface, content, technology, documents, communications, support, and connected providers.

Use the topics below when you create or review work in this area. Follow your repository’s conventions where they are more specific.

## Consumers
