# Haptic Design

> **Reference** · **experimental** · **v0.2.0** · updated 2026-10-28

## Overview

Reference for designing and evaluating touch, tactile materials, physical controls, force feedback, vibration, and embodied experiences across digital products, objects, print, and spaces.

## How to use this guide

Haptic design shapes what people learn and feel through contact and bodily action. A handle reveals how firmly to grip it. A dial makes an increment tangible. A fabric yields, drags, warms, and recovers. A brief vibration can make a digital boundary perceptible. These experiences depend on the body, the object, the action, and the situation together.

Use the topics below when you create or review work in this area. Follow your repository’s conventions where they are more specific.
