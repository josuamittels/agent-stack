# Iconography

> **Reference** · **experimental** · **v0.3.0** · updated 2026-11-11

## Overview

Comprehensive iconography reference for metaphor selection, icon and family construction, optical correction, size and style variants, composition, accessible application, localization, semantic systems, vector implementation, production, testing, and governance.

## How to use this guide

An icon connects a visible form to an object, action, condition, or idea. Its usefulness depends on that connection surviving small size, surrounding content, unfamiliar users, different languages, and imperfect rendering. A beautiful outline can represent the wrong action. A familiar metaphor can disappear at its smallest size. A coherent family can become confusing when its members are applied inconsistently.

Use the topics below when you create or review work in this area. Follow your repository’s conventions where they are more specific.

For chart, map, and diagram decisions, consult [Infographics](/guidelines-and-references/guidelines/aesthetics/infographics) for encoding, scales, and truthful abstraction. This guide retains its own domain authority.
