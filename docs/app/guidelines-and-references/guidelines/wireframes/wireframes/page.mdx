# Wireframe Design Guide

> **Reference** · **experimental** · **v0.1.0** · updated 2026-10-28

## Overview

Reference for creating, comparing, presenting, and exporting rough grayscale interface wireframes at a declared screen size. Use for early interface variants and structural design exploration.

## How to use this guide

Wireframes communicate an interface's structure and direction through rough skeleton blocks. They make placement, hierarchy, contrast, density, and the main action visible while leaving final styling and content open. Aim for a minimalist, aesthetic composition that remains quick to change.

Use the topics below when you create or review work in this area. Follow your repository’s conventions where they are more specific.
