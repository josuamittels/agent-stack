## Outcome and scope

- Users, situation, primary job, and success condition.
- Requested behavior, fixed constraints, affected surfaces, and exclusions.
- Facts and assumptions, their sources, and material unknowns.

## Project evidence

- Relevant routes, feature behavior, permissions, and data constraints, with paths.
- Existing rendered views inspected and their states; source-only inferences and evidence gaps.
- Component APIs/variants, token definitions, assets, and conventions that constrain the plan.
- Research and brand inputs, including limits.

## Selected direction

- Desired experience expressed through hierarchy, density, rhythm, typography, colour, surfaces, and interaction emphasis.
- Applicable aesthetics sections and consequential decisions they informed.
- What carries forward from the current system; justified exceptions and affected consumers.
- Material alternatives considered, selection reason, and tradeoffs.
- Suggested mechanisms changed to better serve the intended outcome; explicit requirements awaiting a decision remain unchanged.

## Journey and layout

- Entry, actions, navigation, success, exit/back, and recovery sequence.
- Link or embed the layout representation with grouping, order, scale, and action emphasis.
- Specify relevant narrow/wide arrangements, resizing rules, and content extremes.
