# Role relevance framework

## Score evidence items

Score each item from 0 to 3 on five dimensions:

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Requirement match | none | adjacent | clear | exact or distinctive |
| Evidence strength | inference | self-description | specific factual or quantified evidence | third-party evidence plus outcome |
| Recency | old or unclear | older | recent | current/recent and central |
| Differentiation | generic | common | uncommon combination | memorable proof |
| Narrative fit | distracts | neutral | supports | anchors positioning |

Total the score out of 15. Normally prioritize evidence scoring 10 or higher; use scores of 8–9 to fill a gap, and usually cut evidence below 8. Treat scores as a selection aid, not a substitute for judgment.

## Role-family emphasis

- **Product management:** discovery, prioritization, cross-functional delivery, product outcomes, analytics, and user experience.
- **Strategy, business analysis, and consulting:** problem diagnosis, metrics, research, recommendations, stakeholder alignment, and structured outputs.
- **Startup operations:** execution under ambiguity, operational change, fundraising or partnerships when evidenced, automation, and broad ownership.
- **AI product and automation:** agent or workflow design, integrations, technical translation, human oversight, and demonstrated product outcomes. Do not imply AI research credentials without evidence.
- **Data and analytics:** data quality, SQL or statistical analysis, modeling, metric definition, dashboards, and decisions informed by analysis.
- **Design and frontend:** research, user flows, prototypes, components, and implementation. Do not present coordination as coding.
- **Finance and investing:** market analysis, business models, financial analysis, diligence, and investing exposure. Do not imply transaction experience without evidence.
- **Large enterprise or risk-sensitive roles:** emphasize governance, decision rights, prioritization, production impact, data quality, dependency coordination, and collaboration across established teams. Do not assume that startup or founder-led work is equivalent to enterprise governance.

These are prompts for evidence selection, not fixed claims about a candidate or universal hiring rules.

## Selection rule

Choose evidence that covers distinct requirements. Do not spend several bullets proving the same strength while leaving other important requirements unaddressed.
